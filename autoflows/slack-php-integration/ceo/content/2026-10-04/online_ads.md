# Online Ads — Display & Earning — 2026-10-04


## EARNING MODELS

- **AFFILIATE** — Affiliate Revenue Share | per referred sale + rev-share | ~$15.00 | platforms: blog,facebook,linkedin
  Commission on referred sales plus recurring revenue share on retainers closed from your links.

- **CPA** — Cost Per Action / Acquisition | per qualified action | ~$22.00 | platforms: facebook,linkedin,blog
  Earn on signup, download or purchase. Highest payout, lowest volume — pair with gated case studies.

- **CPC** — Cost Per Click | per ad click | ~$0.35 | platforms: facebook,youtube,linkedin,blog
  Earn when a visitor clicks a served ad. Best for high-intent blog traffic and mid-funnel social placements.

- **CPM** — Cost Per Mille (per 1000 impressions) | per 1000 impressions | ~$2.80 | platforms: facebook,youtube,linkedin,blog
  Earn on volume of eyes. Strongest on video pre-roll (YouTube) and above-the-fold display banners.

- **CPV** — Cost Per View (video) | per 30s video view | ~$0.12 | platforms: youtube,facebook
  YouTube Partner / in-stream style payout for completed or 30-second qualified views.

- **PROGRAMMATIC** — Programmatic Display (RTB) | blended eCPM | ~$1.90 | platforms: blog,facebook,linkedin
  Real-time-bidding header auction across SSPs; fills unsold inventory automatically.

- **SPONSORED** — Sponsored Post / Native Placement | flat fee per placement | ~$250.00 | platforms: linkedin,facebook,blog
  Flat-fee native advertorial slots sold directly to advertisers — no middleman, highest margin.


## BUYING MODELS

- **PPC** — Paid Search (buying) | per click | ~$1.80
  Outbound spend model for Google Search / LinkedIn Sponsored — used to buy, not earn, traffic.
