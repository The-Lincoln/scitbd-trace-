## 1. Define Your Target Audience
* **Identify High-Intent Sectors:** Segment prospective buyers across SCITBD's 7 primary target industries: **Public Sector** (E-Government, Smart Cities), **Education** (EdTech, LMS), **Healthcare** (Telemedicine, Hospitals), **Commerce/Retail** (POS, E-Commerce), **Non-Profits** (NGOs, Donor Projects), **Enterprises** (Custom ERP/HRM), and **SaaS Startups**.
* **Map Geographic Target Markets (7 Regions):** Focus outreach across 30+ countries on 7 continents, specifically targeting South Asia (Bangladesh HQ), Middle East (UAE, KSA), Europe (UK, Germany, France), North America (USA, Canada), Oceania (Australia, NZ), Southeast Asia (Singapore), and Africa.
* **Identify Key Decision-Makers:** Target specific C-suite and executive personas, including **CTOs, CIOs, Digital Directors, Marketing Leads, and NGO Program Coordinators**.
* **Uncover Core Buyer Pain Points:**
  * *Dark Data & Unused Analytics:* Organizations capturing large datasets but analyzing only ~5%, wasting storage fees while missing growth insights.
  * *Manual Marketing & High Costs:* Excessive time spent on manual social posting, cold outreach, and lead scoring.
  * *Compliance & Security Gaps:* Lack of **ISO 27001 & GDPR compliance** or failure to satisfy strict donor Logical Frameworks (Logframes).
  * *Slow Agency Response Times:* Frustration with traditional agencies taking days or weeks to deliver technical proposals or support responses.

## 2. Develop Ideal Customer Buyer Personas
Map specific buyer profiles directly to SCITBD's service capabilities:

1. **The Middle East / European Enterprise CTO (e.g., UAE, UK, Germany):**

   * **Pain Point:** Security vulnerabilities and slow developer execution.

2. **The US / North American SaaS Founder & SME Director:**
   * **Profile:** Fast-growing business owner seeking automated lead generation and client support.
   * **Pain Point:** High labor costs and bloated manual marketing workflows.
   * **SCITBD Solution:** **Proprietary AI Product Suite** (AI Chatbot Builder, AI Content Generator, AI Business Analytics) engineered to reduce manual marketing effort by 70% and operational costs by 40%.
3. **The Global NGO & Humanitarian Program Director:**
   * **Profile:** Program manager overseeing donor-funded field projects.
   * **Pain Point:** Disconnected spreadsheets, duplicate beneficiary entries, and delayed quarterly donor reporting.
   * **SCITBD Solution:** Audit-ready **Humanitarian M&E & Database Monitoring Software** featuring Result-Based Monitoring (RBM) engines and standardized **SMART, QQTTL, and CREAM** indicator calculators.

## 3. Create Compelling SCITBD Offers & Pricing Architecture
* **Core Value Proposition:** Position SCITBD's digital marketing and IT suite around **"Silicon Valley precision and Dhaka agility"** backed by 24/7 AI-assisted operations.
* **Low-Friction Entry Offers:**
  * **Free 30-Day Enterprise AI Pilot:** Offer 20 exclusive monthly trial slots for enterprise prospects to deploy custom AI Chatbots or Business Analytics Dashboards with zero technical debt.
  * **Free 10-Minute Zoom Technical Consultation:** Direct discovery session with Founder Md Shoeb Lincoln.
  * **Sub-2-Hour Proposal Blueprint:** Customized technical and marketing proposal generated via SCITBD's **Proposal Factory**.
* **Tiered Pricing Architecture (Sabri Suby Method):** Structure services into **3 distinct packages** (Top-tier full enterprise package, Middle-tier core retainer bread-and-butter, and Base-tier entry package) to prevent margin erosion while giving clients options that fit their budget.

## 4. Develop a 24-Hour Multi-Channel Marketing Plan
Align marketing tactics across SCITBD's **24-Hour BST Autonomous Operational Cycle**:

* **SEO & Content Engine:** Publish **3 SEO blog posts daily** and **30 LinkedIn thought-leadership articles monthly** targeting 200 high-intent keywords across 10 service categories.
* **Video Marketing Machine:** Produce **8 short-form videos monthly** (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels) targeting 10,000 views per video to demonstrate software features and platform demos.
* **Automated Paid Ads & ROAS Audit:** Run Google Search Ads, Facebook Carousel Ads, and LinkedIn Sponsored Content. Automatically **pause any campaign showing 3 consecutive days of negative ROAS**.
* **24-Hour Time-Block Execution (BST / UTC+6):**
  * **Block 1 (06:00 – 12:00 BST):** *South Asia & BD Focus* — Deploy morning SEO blogs, scan local e-GP government tender portals, and execute lead-nurturing workflows.
  * **Block 2 (12:00 – 18:00 BST):** *Middle East & Europe Focus* — Perform ad ROAS audits, initiate B2B outreach to 10–12 CTOs daily via LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and publish European SEO content.
  * **Block 3 (18:00 – 00:00 BST):** *UK & North America Focus* — Launch US/Canada ad campaigns, process incoming inquiries within the **sub-2-hour proposal SLA**, and execute $10,000+ CEO video escalations.
  * **Block 4 (00:00 – 06:00 BST):** *Oceania & Global Reboot* — Reallocate ad budgets dynamically, initiate Australian B2B outreach, compile the Daily CEO Intelligence Briefing, and reset system queues.

## 6. Develop the Sales Funnel (Sabri Suby Discovery-First Model)
* **Top of Funnel (Awareness):** Attract buyers via high-intent SEO blog posts, LinkedIn authority content, short-form video demos, and targeted paid ads.
* **Middle of Funnel (Consideration & Diagnosis):** Engage prospects through gated case study PDFs, free 30-day AI chatbot trials, and an **automated 7-touch email drip sequence** over 21 days.
* **Bottom of Funnel (Closing - Sabri Suby Framework):**
  * **Diagnosis-First Discovery:** Spend 80% of sales calls listening to diagnose exact client bottlenecks ("sell like a doctor" prescribing a custom technical solution).
  * **Sub-2-Hour Technical Proposal SLA:** Generate and dispatch tailored technical proposals within 2 hours of inquiry.
  * **$10,000+ CEO Video Escalation Protocol:** Any inbound deal valued at ≥ $10,000 USD triggers a personalized **CEO video message** sent within 24 hours.
  * **Assign Cost of Inaction:** Overcome "maybe" objections by framing the financial and competitive cost of doing nothing vs. moving forward.
* **Post-Sale Retention & Retainer Conversion:** Run automated upsell workflows to convert **30% of single-project clients into monthly retainer agreements** (IT Support AMC, Cloud Management, Digital Marketing).
